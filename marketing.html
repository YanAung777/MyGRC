Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about AI standards, workspaces, or exporting evidence? Find immediate clarity below.

What is ISO 42001, and how does C&A Portal help?

ISO 42001 is the international standard specifying requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS). C&A Portal translates these abstract controls into practical, bite-sized tasks, allowing your security team to map model inventory, verify compliance checklists, and secure auditing evidence effortlessly.

How do multi-workspaces work for consulting firms?

If you are a consultancy firm or managing multiple child organizations, you can spin up dedicated, isolated environments (called 'Workspaces') inside C&A Portal. Each workspace holds its own unique task statuses, policy definitions, model registry, and custom compliance reports. This ensures complete client confidentiality.

Can we export compliance reports into PDF/DOCX format?

Yes. Once your compliance checklists are filled and your safety evidence is loaded, you can compile and download full bundles in clean, auditor-ready formats in a single click. These reports are perfectly structured to serve as primary artifacts for certifying registrars.

How secure is the data stored on C&A Portal?

C&A Portal runs inside high-security containers utilizing strict 256-bit sandbox constraints. All evidence files and metadata are encrypted both in transit and at rest, meaning external bad actors can never access your organization's confidential model logs or custom GRC strategies.
